# SMART COURT | AI-Powered Case Management & Judicial Analytics Platform

A state-of-the-art, institutional, and secure judicial technology dashboard developed with React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts, and Lucide Icons.

---

## 🏛️ Features Overview

1. **Judicial Auth & Multi-Role Simulator**: Pre-configured login and top-bar role switcher for **Court Administrator, Hon'ble Judge, Advocate / Lawyer, Registry Clerk, and Citizen / Litigant**.
2. **Main Judicial Dashboard**: High-density KPI cards, Recharts Case Overview & Status Distribution charts, Urgent Priority Cases table, and live AI Judicial Insights panel.
3. **Case Management System (CMS)**: Comprehensive case ledger with multi-filtering, sorting, pagination, and multi-step **"+ Register New Case"** wizard with auto-CNR generation.
4. **Deep-Dive 8-Tab Case Dossier**: Overview, Parties & Advocates, Hearings Ledger, Documents & Evidence, Visual Milestone Pipeline, AI Legal Analysis, Precedent Citations, and Immutable Audit Trail.
5. **AI Document Intelligence**: Drag-and-drop OCR and NLP document classification (*Petition, Affidavit, Evidence, Judgment, Order, Notice, Application*) with entity extraction.
6. **Sensitive Data Redaction Suite**: Detection and masking of PII (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Accounts, Phone, Emails, Addresses, Minors' data) with Mask Mode / Audit Mode and clean export.
7. **AI Case Timeline & Delay Risk Prediction**: Estimated completion dates, delay risk probability (Low/Moderate/High), and procedural bottleneck diagnostics.
8. **Hearing Schedule Optimizer**: Courtrooms 1–6 daily cause list matrix with live conflict detection and 1-click **"Apply Suggested Schedule"**.
9. **Judicial Workload & Pendency Analytics**: Bench workload distribution, 5-year historical backlog reduction curve, and courtroom velocity benchmarks.
10. **Legal Precedent Retrieval Engine**: Semantic search across landmark Supreme Court & High Court citations, ratio decidendi extraction, and automated AI Legal Bench Memo generator.
11. **Reports & Cause Lists Center**: Official printable cause list layout, PDF export, Excel export, and print formatting.
12. **Dedicated Citizen Portal**: 16-digit CNR case tracking, plain-language 6-stage milestone tracker, certified copy requests, and SMS/WhatsApp alerts.
13. **Lawyer / Advocate Practice Chamber**: Daily listed matters, 1-click **Join Virtual Courtroom (VC)** link, and draft e-filings.
14. **Audit & Cryptographic Security**: Immutable event logs sealed with SHA-256 integrity hashes.
15. **NyayaBot AI Judicial Assistant**: Omnipresent floating legal administrative assistant.
